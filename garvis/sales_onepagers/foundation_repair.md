### The situation you're in
You bid and plan repairs largely blind — diagnosing movement on arrival, sometimes re-mobilizing when the picture changes. And once a repair is done, you have no easy way to *prove* it's holding. Garvis fixes both ends of the job.

- **Faster, tighter bids** — targeted scopes instead of padded estimates; fewer surprises and re-mobilizations.
- **Post-repair verification** — sensor data proves the structure has stabilized, protecting your warranty and reputation.
- **A recurring revenue upsell** — leave continuous monitoring in place after the repair; earn ongoing margin and share.
- **More referrals** — customers and their engineers trust data-backed work.
