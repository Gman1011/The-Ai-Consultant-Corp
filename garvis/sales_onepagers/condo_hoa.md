# Garvis for Condo & HOA Boards

**Protect your residents and your reserves — between inspections, not just during them.**

---

### The situation you're in
Under Florida **SB 4-D**, your building faces milestone structural inspections and a reserve study — and your residents are watching their assessments. But a formal inspection is a single moment in time. What happens to your foundation, columns, and sea wall in the *years between* inspections is invisible — until it becomes a surprise, an emergency, or a special assessment.

### What Garvis does for you
Discreet wireless sensors install in **minutes** with no disruption to residents, then monitor your building **24/7**. You get a simple **Green / Yellow / Orange / Red** health score, a clear monthly report your board can actually read, and an **instant alert** if something changes.

### Why boards choose Garvis
- **Resident trust & transparency** — show owners you are watching the building continuously, not just every several years.
- **Fewer surprises** — catch movement, settlement, and water intrusion early, while repairs are smaller and planned.
- **Compliance confidence** — a continuous structural record that complements your engineer's milestone work.
- **Protects reserves** — proactive, targeted maintenance beats reactive emergency repair and surprise assessments.
- **Supports your engineer** — Garvis informs licensed engineering judgment; it never replaces it.

### How it works
1. Quick sensor plan designed for your building (foundation, columns, sea wall, garage).
2. Minutes-long, non-disruptive install.
3. Live dashboard + monthly board report + automatic alerts.
4. Your engineer can access the same data when it's time to act.

### The bottom line
**Know earlier. Respond faster. Protect more.** Give your board, your residents, and your engineer continuous eyes on the building most of your owners' net worth is tied up in.
