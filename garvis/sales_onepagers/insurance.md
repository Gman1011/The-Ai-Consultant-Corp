# Garvis for Insurance Carriers & Reinsurers

**Underwrite with forward-looking structural behavior. Settle claims with history.**

---

### The situation you're in
Florida is the toughest property-insurance market in the country. You're pricing structural risk on snapshots, third-party reports, and the past — with limited forward-looking signal on how a given building is actually behaving. After a storm, claims are slow and contested because no one has the structure's pre-event history.

### What Garvis gives underwriting & claims
- **Forward-looking risk data** — continuous movement, settlement, tilt, vibration, and water-intrusion behavior per insured structure.
- **Better risk selection & pricing** — distinguish a stable building from a quietly deteriorating one *before* binding.
- **Faster, defensible claims** — a timestamped behavioral history (movement, weather, event log, settlement curve) at the moment a claim is filed.
- **Mitigation programs** — offer premium credits for continuously monitored, demonstrably maintained buildings.
- **Portfolio visibility** — a live map of monitored risk across your book.

### How it works
1. Start with a **portfolio pilot** on a defined set of insured buildings to quantify the data's value.
2. Garvis delivers dashboards and a structured **data feed / API** into your underwriting and claims workflows.
3. Expand into a mitigation-credit program that rewards monitored, well-maintained structures.

### Why this matters now
Post-Surfside scrutiny, SB 4-D mandates, and reinsurance pressure all reward carriers who can demonstrate better structural-risk data. Garvis turns continuous structural intelligence into an underwriting and claims advantage — and a customer-retention story.

### The bottom line
Replace snapshot guesswork with continuous, auditable structural behavior — for smarter underwriting and faster, more defensible claims.
