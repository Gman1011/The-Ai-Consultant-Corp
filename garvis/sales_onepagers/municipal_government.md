# Garvis for Municipal & County Governments

**Portfolio-wide structural safety, on one live map.**

---

### The situation you're in
You're responsible for the public's safety across a portfolio of aging assets — schools, fire stations, hospitals, government buildings, bridges, sea walls, parking garages — in a region defined by hurricanes, flooding, and coastal corrosion. Inspections are periodic and resource-limited, and after Surfside the public expects proactive assurance, not reactive response.

### What Garvis gives your agency
- **One live command-center map** — every monitored structure shown as an icon with a real-time **Green / Yellow / Orange / Red** health score.
- **Early warning** — when a building shifts from Green to Yellow, an automatic alert triggers an inspection request *before* a problem becomes a failure.
- **Proactive capital planning** — prioritize repair budgets by actual structural behavior, not just age or the squeakiest wheel.
- **Storm-time awareness** — real-time structural impact across critical facilities during and after a hurricane.
- **Public-safety narrative** — demonstrate measurable, continuous diligence to residents and elected leadership.
- **Supports your engineers** — Garvis informs city/county engineering judgment; it never replaces it.

### How it works
1. Start with a high-visibility pilot — a bridge, a cluster of schools, or a fire station.
2. Garvis deploys sensors and stands up the portfolio dashboard with alerts and SLAs.
3. Expand across the facility portfolio; integrate with emergency-management workflows.

### Funding paths
Capital facility budgets, resilience appropriations, and federal/state grants (including emergency-management and infrastructure-resilience programs) can support deployment. We help structure pilots to fit.

### The bottom line
Turn periodic, reactive inspection into continuous, portfolio-wide structural intelligence — and give leadership a credible public-safety story backed by live data.
