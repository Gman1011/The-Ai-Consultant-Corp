## 1. Executive Summary

Garvis Monitoring Intelligence ("Garvis") is an IoT structural health monitoring (SHM) platform purpose-built for South Florida's buildings and infrastructure. Garvis installs discreet, wireless sensors on foundations, columns, beams, sea walls, parking garages, and bridges, then streams continuous data to a cloud platform that scores each structure's health as **Green / Yellow / Orange / Red** and alerts stakeholders the moment behavior changes.

Traditional inspections capture a **moment**. Garvis captures the **story** — the trend between inspections, where failures actually develop.

**Why now, why here.** South Florida is the highest-stakes structural monitoring market in the United States:

- The 2021 **Surfside (Champlain Towers South) collapse** killed 98 people and permanently changed the regulatory and insurance landscape.
- Florida **Senate Bill 4-D (2022)** now mandates **Milestone Structural Inspections** for condominiums 3+ stories at 30 years (25 years within 3 miles of the coast) and recurring **Structural Integrity Reserve Studies (SIRS)**.
- Florida's **property-insurance crisis** has driven premiums to the highest in the nation and pushed carriers to demand better risk data.
- The region combines **hurricanes, storm surge, chronic flooding, saltwater intrusion, sea-level rise, expansive/limestone soils, and rapidly aging 1970s–80s coastal towers** — a uniquely severe risk stack.

**The opportunity.** Garvis sits between the formal inspection (required by law, performed by licensed engineers every several years) and the unknown that happens in between. We are not a replacement for engineers — we are the continuous early-warning layer that makes their judgment timelier and cheaper to apply.

**Business model.** Hardware + recurring SaaS. Sensors are sold or leased per structure; the platform is billed as a per-building/per-sensor monthly subscription with tiered service levels. Revenue compounds through multi-year monitoring contracts and channel partnerships with engineering firms, foundation-repair companies, and insurers.

**The ask / objective.** Establish Garvis as the category-defining continuous SHM platform in the tri-county South Florida market (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach) within 24–36 months, then expand along the Gulf and Atlantic coasts.

## 2. Company & Product Overview

### 2.1 Mission
Protect buildings, infrastructure, and the communities that depend on them by making structural change visible the moment it happens.

### 2.2 Vision
A Florida — and ultimately a coastline — where every critical structure can tell us when something changes, before damage becomes danger or expense.

### 2.3 What Garvis Does
Garvis is an end-to-end platform across three layers:

1. **Edge (Sensors).** Low-power wireless IoT sensors that mount discreetly and install in minutes with no structural disruption. They measure:
   - Ground settlement & foundation movement
   - Building tilt & differential movement
   - Structural vibration (traffic, construction, seismic micro-events)
   - Crack initiation & propagation
   - Micro-displacement & real-time strain
   - Humidity, temperature, water intrusion
   - Hydrostatic pressure & storm/wind loading

2. **Connectivity (Cloud Ingest).** Cellular/LoRa connectivity streams data continuously into the Garvis Cloud — 24 hours a day, 365 days a year — with offline buffering for storm conditions.

3. **Intelligence (Platform).** Dashboards, trend analysis, movement vectors, settlement curves, vibration spectra, weather overlays, AI-assisted anomaly detection, predictive-maintenance indicators, automated reporting, and a simple **Green/Yellow/Orange/Red Health Score** that any stakeholder can understand at a glance. Automatic alerts reach engineers, owners, cities, and insurers instantly.

### 2.4 Core Positioning Principles
- **Continuous, not snapshot.** Monitors the trend between formal inspections.
- **Decision-grade, not just data.** Health scoring and alerts, not raw telemetry dumps.
- **Supports engineers, never replaces them.** Garvis informs licensed engineering judgment; it does not certify structures.
- **Built for South Florida.** Hurricanes, flooding, coastal corrosion, aging stock, and the SB 4-D regulatory regime are designed into the product, not bolted on.
- **A permanent structural record.** Every monitored asset accumulates an auditable behavioral history valuable to owners, engineers, insurers, and regulators.

## 3. Market Analysis

### 3.1 Macro Drivers (South Florida)
- **Regulatory mandate.** SB 4-D milestone inspections + SIRS create a recurring, legally required engagement cycle for thousands of condo associations — and a natural attach point for continuous monitoring.

- **Aging inventory.** A large share of oceanfront condos were built in the 1970s–1980s and are now hitting 30–50 year recertification milestones simultaneously.
- **Environmental load.** Sea-level rise, king tides, saltwater intrusion, and hurricane frequency accelerate deterioration of coastal foundations and sea walls.
- **Public-safety politics.** Post-Surfside, elected officials, municipalities, and emergency managers are highly motivated to demonstrate proactive structural safety.

## 4. Problem & Solution

**Problem.** Buildings fail gradually, then suddenly. Between mandated inspections — often years apart — structures move, crack, settle, and corrode invisibly. By the time the next inspection or a visible symptom appears, the cheapest moment to act has passed, and in rare catastrophic cases lives are lost. Owners lack data; engineers lack continuity; insurers lack forward-looking risk; cities lack portfolio-wide visibility.

**Solution.** Garvis makes the invisible visible, continuously. It detects the *trend* — rate and direction of movement — not just a single reading. It routes a clear health signal and instant alerts to everyone who needs to act, turning reactive emergency repair into proactive, planned, targeted maintenance.

## 5. Business Model & Pricing

### 5.1 Revenue Streams
1. **Hardware** — sensor kits per structure (sold or leased). Lease lowers the buyer's upfront barrier and increases recurring revenue.
2. **SaaS subscription** — recurring monitoring fee per building (and/or per sensor), tiered by service level.
3. **Professional services** — installation, sensor-plan design, integration with engineering reports, custom dashboards.
4. **Data & reporting products** — compliance-ready reports, insurer data feeds, portfolio dashboards for cities/REITs.
5. **Channel/partner revenue share** — referral and reseller economics with engineering firms, foundation companies, and insurers.

### 5.2 Tiered Packaging (illustrative)
| Tier | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | Single small building / sea wall | Core sensors, Health Score, monthly report, basic alerts |
| **Professional** | Condo towers, commercial | Full sensor suite, live dashboard, daily reporting, weather overlays, engineer access, priority alerts |
| **Enterprise / Portfolio** | Municipalities, REITs, insurers | Multi-asset map dashboard, API/data feed, SLAs, predictive analytics, dedicated success manager |

### 5.3 Unit-Economics Logic
- **Land** with a single building (often triggered by SB 4-D timing or a recent assessment dispute).
- **Expand** to additional buildings in the same association, management company, or portfolio.
- **Retain** via multi-year monitoring contracts; switching cost rises as the structural history accumulates (the record only gets more valuable over time).
- Channel partners reduce CAC by embedding Garvis into work already being sold (inspections, repairs, underwriting).

## 8. Key Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Liability / over-promising** ("Garvis missed a failure") | Clear positioning: decision-support, not certification; engineer-in-the-loop; documented SLAs and disclaimers; conservative alerting. |
| **False alarms erode trust** | Trend-based scoring with tuned thresholds; engineer review workflow before red escalation. |
| **Hardware reliability in storms/corrosion** | Marine-grade, low-power, offline-buffering sensors; redundancy on critical assets; field-tested install playbook. |
| **Long public-sector sales cycles** | Lead with private condo/commercial revenue; use pilots + grants for public sector. |
| **Incumbent / new entrants** | Speed-to-deploy, SB 4-D specialization, channel lock-in, accumulating data moat. |
| **Data security & privacy** | Encryption, access controls, role-based dashboards, auditable records. |
| **Regulatory change** | Stay close to legislators/engineering boards; design to exceed evolving standards. |
