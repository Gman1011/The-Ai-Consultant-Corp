# Garvis Monitoring Intelligence — Investor Teaser

**Continuous Structural Intelligence for South Florida's Buildings & Infrastructure**
*Know Earlier. Respond Faster. Protect More.*

---

### The one-liner
Garvis is an IoT structural-health-monitoring platform that watches buildings, bridges, and sea walls **continuously** — and translates movement, settlement, and stress into a simple **Green→Red health score** with instant alerts — starting in the highest-stakes structural market in the U.S.: South Florida.

### The problem
Buildings fail gradually, then suddenly. Mandated inspections are years apart and capture a single moment. Between them, structures move, crack, settle, and corrode invisibly — and the cheapest (sometimes the only safe) window to act closes unseen. Owners lack data, engineers lack continuity, insurers lack forward-looking risk, and cities lack portfolio-wide visibility.

### The solution
Discreet wireless sensors install in minutes with no disruption and stream data 24/7 to the Garvis Cloud. We detect the **trend** — rate and direction of movement — not just a single reading, and route a clear health signal plus instant alerts to every stakeholder. **Garvis supports licensed engineers; it never replaces engineering judgment.**

### Why now, why here
- **Regulatory mandate:** Florida **SB 4-D** requires milestone structural inspections (30 yrs / 25 yrs near coast) + recurring reserve studies for thousands of condos.
- **Post-Surfside urgency:** the 2021 Champlain Towers collapse permanently reset safety expectations.
- **Insurance crisis:** the nation's highest premiums and carriers demanding better risk data.
- **Aging, exposed inventory:** 1970s–80s coastal towers hitting recertification *en masse*, amid hurricanes, flooding, saltwater intrusion, and sea-level rise.

> Regulatory mandate + insurance pressure + aging stock + environmental load all peak in the **same region at the same time.**

### Business model
Hardware (sold or leased) **+ recurring SaaS** per building, tiered (Essential / Professional / Enterprise-Portfolio), plus services, data products, and channel revenue share. **Land** one building → **expand** across the association/portfolio → **retain** via multi-year contracts whose switching cost rises as the structural record accumulates.

### Go-to-market
**Channel-led:** engineers (continuity + differentiation), foundation companies (targeted repairs + post-repair monitoring), and insurers (risk data + pilots) become distribution — embedding Garvis into work already being sold. A public-sector pilot track (a bridge, schools, a fire station) builds the safety narrative and unlocks budgets/grants.
