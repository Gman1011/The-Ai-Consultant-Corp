# Garvis Monitoring Intelligence — Pitch Deck Outline

A slide-by-slide outline for a ~12–15 slide investor deck. Each slide lists the **headline**, the **content**, and the **visual** (built in the cinematic Apple-keynote / NASA-engineering aesthetic). Keep one idea per slide.

---

### Slide 1 — Title
- **Headline:** Garvis Monitoring Intelligence — *Continuous Structural Intelligence*
- **Content:** Tagline "Know Earlier. Respond Faster. Protect More." · presenter · date.
- **Visual:** Drone shot of the South Florida skyline at dusk with subtle HUD overlays; Garvis logo.

### Slide 2 — The hook / why this matters
- **Headline:** Buildings fail gradually — then suddenly.
- **Content:** Post-Surfside reality in one or two lines; the cost of finding out too late (safety, money, trust).
- **Visual:** A single building with an animated Green→Yellow→Red transition.

### Slide 3 — The problem
- **Headline:** Inspections capture a moment. The danger lives in between.
- **Content:** Mandated inspections are years apart; structures move/crack/settle/corrode invisibly between them. Owners lack data, engineers lack continuity, insurers lack forward risk, cities lack visibility.
- **Visual:** Split image — clipboard inspection (one snapshot) vs. a continuous live graph.

### Slide 4 — Why now, why here
- **Headline:** Four forces peak in one region at the same time.
- **Content:** SB 4-D mandate · post-Surfside urgency · insurance crisis · aging + exposed coastal inventory (hurricanes, flooding, saltwater intrusion, sea-level rise).
- **Visual:** 2×2 quadrant of the four drivers converging on a map of South Florida.

### Slide 5 — The solution
- **Headline:** Garvis watches every structure, 24/7.
- **Content:** Wireless sensors → Garvis Cloud → decision-grade **Green/Yellow/Orange/Red** health score + instant alerts. Detects the *trend*, not just a reading. **Supports engineers; never replaces them.**
- **Visual:** Three-layer diagram (Edge → Cloud → Intelligence) + the Health Score dial.

### Slide 6 — Product / how it works
- **Headline:** Minutes to install. A lifetime of structural memory.
- **Content:** What it measures (settlement, tilt, vibration, crack propagation, strain, water intrusion, hydrostatic/storm/wind loading). Live dashboards, weather overlays, predictive indicators, automated reports.
- **Visual:** 3D cutaway of a building with sensors lighting up; dashboard screenshots.

### Slide 7 — Demo / the "story" vs the "snapshot"
- **Headline:** Traditional inspections capture moments. Garvis captures the story.
- **Content:** A real settlement curve over time with an alert firing before the next scheduled inspection.
- **Visual:** Animated timeline graph with an alert marker and a phone notification.

### Slide 8 — Market
- **Headline:** The highest-stakes structural market in the U.S.
- **Content:** 8 buyer segments; tri-county beachhead; TAM/SAM/SOM. *(Insert verified figures.)*
- **Visual:** Concentric TAM/SAM/SOM rings + segment icons.

### Slide 9 — Business model
- **Headline:** Hardware + compounding recurring SaaS.
- **Content:** Sold/leased sensors + per-building subscription (Essential / Professional / Enterprise-Portfolio) + services + data + channel share. Land → expand → retain; switching cost rises with the record.
- **Visual:** Pricing-tier table + a land/expand/retain flywheel.

### Slide 10 — Go-to-market
- **Headline:** Turn the ecosystem into our salesforce.
- **Content:** Channel-led via engineers, foundation companies, and insurers; public-sector pilot track for the safety narrative and grants.
- **Visual:** Channel diagram with Garvis at the center feeding owners, engineers, insurers, cities.

### Slide 11 — Why we win / moat
- **Headline:** Speed, specialization, and an accumulating data moat.
- **Content:** Minutes-long installs · SB 4-D / South Florida specialization · multi-stakeholder dashboards · channel lock-in · structural-history data moat.
- **Visual:** Competitive matrix (Garvis vs. legacy SHM vs. manual inspection vs. generic IoT).

### Slide 12 — Traction & roadmap
- **Headline:** From reference sites to category leader.
- **Content:** Design partners → reference deployments → first paying associations + municipal pilot → published case studies; 4-phase roadmap.
- **Visual:** Timeline with phase milestones.

### Slide 13 — Financials
- **Headline:** A compounding recurring-revenue curve.
- **Content:** Illustrative 3-year revenue, gross-margin expansion, exit ARR; key sensitivities (lease vs. sell, channel mix, churn). *(From financial_model.csv — mark illustrative.)*
- **Visual:** Revenue + ARR bar/line chart; margin trend.

### Slide 14 — Team
- **Headline:** The people to build the structural-safety standard.
- **Content:** Founders/leadership, domain advisors (structural engineering, insurance, IoT/hardware, gov), key hires.
- **Visual:** Headshots + one-line credibility per person.

### Slide 15 — The ask & vision
- **Headline:** Imagine a Florida where every critical structure can tell us when something changes.
- **Content:** Round size, use of funds, milestones it unlocks. Close on the brand promise.
- **Visual:** Blue digital monitoring network spreading across the Florida map; logo + "Know Earlier. Respond Faster. Protect More."
